Getting paid with Tranxact: payment links, QR codes, and tips

August 2026

If you sell anything: a service, a product, your time, or you just want people to be able to support you, Tranxact gives you a few real ways to collect money. Here's exactly how each one works.

Payment links

A payment link is the simplest way to get paid. You create one from your dashboard at pay.tranxact.co, give it a title, and set either a fixed amount or leave it flexible so the person paying can enter their own amount.

Once it's created, you get a real, shareable link. Drop it in a WhatsApp chat, an invoice, an email, anywhere. Whoever opens it sees a clean checkout page and can pay by bank transfer or supported cryptocurrency, no account required on their end.

QR codes

Every payment link automatically comes with a QR code too. This is the fastest option for in-person payments. A customer scans it with their phone camera and lands straight on the same checkout page. No typing, no app to install.

Tip Me links

If you're a creator, freelancer, or just have an audience that wants to support you directly, a Tip Me link is built for exactly that. It's a single link you can put in your bio and leave open indefinitely: there's no need to create a new request every time someone wants to send something your way.

How settlement actually works

However someone pays, bank transfer or crypto, the money lands in the same place: your Tranxact wallet balance. If it comes in as crypto, it's converted to naira automatically at the time of payment, so you're not left holding a separate crypto balance to manage.

Every payment is tracked and logged, so you always have a clear record of what came in, from where, and when.
