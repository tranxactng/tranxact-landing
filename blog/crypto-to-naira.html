How Tranxact turns crypto into naira, automatically

August 2026

Tranxact supports receiving seven cryptocurrencies: BTC, ETH, BNB, USDT, USDC, TRX, and SOL. Here's what actually happens when someone sends you one of them.

You get a real address, not a shared one

Every user gets their own unique deposit address per coin, the same as any wallet you'd use elsewhere. When crypto arrives at that address, it's detected and matched to your account.

Automatic conversion

Rather than sitting in your account as a separate crypto balance you'd need to manage or withdraw separately, it's converted to naira at the time it's received and credited straight to your main wallet balance. The naira value is based on the real market rate at the moment of conversion.

One balance, not several

This is the part that actually matters day to day: you don't end up juggling a naira balance and a crypto balance separately. Whatever comes in, a bank transfer, a payment link, or crypto from anywhere in the world, it's all just money in your one Tranxact balance, ready to spend or send.

Multiple networks, where it matters

For coins that exist on more than one network, like USDT, which can move over TRC20, ERC20, or BEP20, Tranxact lets you choose which one you're receiving on, so the address you're given actually matches what's being sent.
