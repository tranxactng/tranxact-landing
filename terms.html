Payment links without an account

You can create a payment link with just your email address and a Nigerian bank account, without signing in to Tranxact. If you do, this section applies to you.

  • We confirm your email with a code, and the name on your bank account must match the name you give us. We check it with your bank.
  • Until you sign in to Tranxact, you can receive up to ₦200,000 in total. When you reach that amount your links are paused, and anything already received is still paid out.
  • When someone pays your link, we send the money to your bank account, less our per-payment fee and the payout fee. Payouts can take time to arrive, and we may hold or review a payout where we need to check it.
  • You are responsible for the bank details you give us. If they are wrong, a payout may fail or go to the wrong account, so please check them carefully.
  • If you later sign in to Tranxact with the same email, your links and payment history become part of your Tranxact account, and new payments are credited to your Tranxact balance instead of being paid out to your bank.

Limits and verification
